The short version

Your voice note is used to make a summary, then it is gone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What happens to a voice note

  • On your device, the audio is converted to a smaller format.
  • It is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server on Cloudflare, which runs a speech-to-text model and a summary model on Cloudflare Workers AI.
  • The transcript and summary are sent back to your device. Neither the audio, the transcript nor the summary is saved on the server.
  • Cloudflare processes the request only to return the result. Under Cloudflare's terms, Workers AI does not use your inputs to train models.

What stays on your device

Your options (summary language, spoken language, sender's name) are remembered in your browser or app storage. Clearing app data removes them.

Permissions in the Android app

The app needs internet access to make summaries. It receives audio only when you share a voice note to it. It does not read your chats, contacts or files.

Children

The service is not aimed at children under 13.
