Made for how families actually talk

Transcripts exist. Nobody wants to read six minutes either. So this gives you the part you need to act on.

  • Urdu, Punjabi and English in one note

    Switches mid-sentence like Khala does. You get the summary in English and Roman Urdu.

  • Pulls out dates, places, who brings what

    “Friday, Lahore, Baji brings biryani, pick Khala up at 4” — listed separately so nothing gets missed.

  • Long notes, short reading

    Up to 30 minutes per note. Several notes in a row are summarized one after another.

  • Nothing is saved

    No account, no history on a server. Audio is turned into text, summarized and dropped.

  • Full transcript when you want it

    Copy the whole text, or share just the three lines with the person who missed it.

  • Free, and quick

    A six-minute note takes about twenty seconds. Works on cheap phones and slow data.

Questions

How do I summarize a WhatsApp voice note?

On Android, install the app, then long-press the voice note in WhatsApp, tap Share and choose Voice Note Summarizer. On any phone or computer, open the online app, pick the voice note file and tap Summarize. You get three lines, a Roman Urdu summary and ready replies.

Does it understand Urdu and Punjabi?

Yes. It is built for notes that mix Urdu, Punjabi and English in the same sentence, which is how most family voice notes sound. Summaries come out in English and Roman Urdu.

Is it free?

Yes. The Android APK and the online app are free, with no sign-up.

Are my voice notes saved?

No. Audio is sent over an encrypted connection, turned into text, summarized and then discarded. Nothing is stored on a server and there are no accounts. Read the privacy policy.

How long can a voice note be?

Up to 30 minutes per note. A typical six-minute note is summarized in under half a minute.

How do I install the APK?

Tap Download APK, open the file, allow installs from your browser when Android asks, then tap Install. After that, Voice Note Summarizer appears in the WhatsApp share menu.
